Our Story & Vision

Lifeline Healthcare was founded by Mr. M.R. Abijith with a singular vision: to bridge the gap between quality pharmaceutical manufacturers and the patients who need them. As a premier pharma marketing and distribution company, we ensure that life-saving medicines are accessible across every corner of our territory.

Over the years, our commitment to ethics and uncompromising quality has helped us build a robust network of stockists and retail pharmacies. Today, we are proud to be the trusted partner for both healthcare professionals prescribing our brands, and retailers stocking them.
