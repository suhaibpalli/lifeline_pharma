Return & Refund Policy

At Lifeline Healthcare, we strive to ensure that all our pharmaceutical products reach you in the best condition. However, we understand that situations may arise where a return or refund is necessary. Please read our policy carefully.

1. Return Eligibility

Returns are generally accepted under the following conditions:

  • The product received is damaged, defective, or tampered with at the time of delivery.
  • The product delivered does not match your order (wrong item).
  • The product has passed its expiration date before delivery.
  • For retail/B2B stockists, specific near-expiry return arrangements are handled as per individual distribution agreements.

Note: Due to regulatory and safety reasons, we cannot accept returns for temperature-sensitive items, opened medicines, or products whose original packaging/seals have been broken.

2. Timeframe for Returns

Any discrepancies, damages, or requests for returns must be reported to our customer support within 48 hours of delivery. Late requests will not be entertained for safety and compliance reasons.

3. Refund Process

Once your return request is approved and the returned item is received at our facility:

  1. Our quality assurance team will inspect the product.
  2. If the claim is found to be valid, a replacement will be dispatched, or a full refund will be initiated.
  3. Refunds will be processed to the original method of payment within 5-7 business days.

4. Order Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before they are dispatched from our warehouse. Once dispatched, cancellations are not permitted. If you need to cancel an order, please contact our support team immediately.
